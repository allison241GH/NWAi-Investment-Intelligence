## The Three Intelligence Tracks

### Track 1 — Deal Intelligence *(Activation)*

*Where deals come from · how we evaluate them*

| Sources | Function |
|---|---|
| Dealum (passive inflow — sunset by YE) | Intake & ingestion |
| Member referrals | Classification (verticals) |
| Cross-syndication (inbound fast-pass) | **AI-powered Screening / Scout / Diligence** |
| Direct founder outreach | **Syndication posture — inbound fast-pass for now** |

**Build sequence:** Activates after substrate ships. Plugs into Track 2 — routing, conviction, ledger writes all flow through the member graph.

**Note on Track 4 (Syndication):** Modeled in v1.0 as *inbound consumption only* — fast-pass through the pipeline when peer-syndicate pre-diligence is present. Lives as a Source and Function inside Track 1. Outbound syndication (NWA leading and bringing peers along) is named conceptually but not architecturally specified in v1.0.

---

### Track 2 — Member/Social Intelligence ★ *(The Substrate)*

*Engaging our Members · SMEs · compounding their judgment*

| Components | Function |
|---|---|
| Member expertise & domain knowledge | Pre-diligence filtering & routing |
| Past investments & track record | Deal-to-member matching by expertise |
| **Member comments + SME POV (across Screen / Scout / Diligence)** | **DD team assembly & conviction building** |
| Participation & conviction signals | **Closed loop — ledger re-weights routing each cycle** |
| **Decisions ledger — the institutional memory** | |

**Build sequence:** **First — Substrate Build.** Member graph · deal card · SME POV capture · decisions ledger · closed loop end-to-end.

**OUR MOAT — Compoundable Member SME Judgment + Relationships.** Designed to make the network's expertise legible, durable, and compounding. No one can copy — every angel network has *some* expertise, but only NWAi compounds it as an architectural property of the platform.

---

### Track 3 — Ecosystem Network Intelligence *(Activation)*

*Where will future deals come from?*

| Sources | Function |
|---|---|
| Incubators & accelerators | Proactive sourcing (agents) |
| Universities & research | Pattern detection |
| Founder & operator networks | Thematic insights & reports |
| Market trends & signals | Pipeline creation upstream of Track 1 |

**Build sequence:** Activates after substrate ships. Plugs into Track 2 — market signals enrich the member graph and produce upstream deal flow into Track 1.

## NWAi Platform V1 — TechGroup MVP First

### V1 Scope

- **TechGroup AI-powered screening, scout, diligence**
- Member profile + expertise graph
- Social deal card (per active deal)
- Member comments + SME POV across **Screen / Scout / Diligence**
- Interactions write to **decisions ledger**
- Ledger **re-weights routing** on the next cycle

> **Enterprise grade platform | Web application | Member accessible | MVP overlay with Dealum for deal-member DB**

### Rollout Cadence

| Phase | Scope | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | TechGroup MVP — soft launch with self-selected members | **30–45 days** |
| **2** | Next group rollout (sequence TBD: Medical / Space / Consumer / Industrial / Fintech) | **+2 weeks** |
| **3** | Per-group rollout cadence — one group at a time | **+2 weeks each** |
| **4** | All 6 groups live on substrate | *Total arc ~3–4 months from V1 ship* |
