Agent-Era Readiness

Our third always-active investing lens — is the founder solving the problem as agents will reshape it?
New World Angels · Investment Intelligence
TechGroup Co-Chair Brief · July 2026
The Shift: AI agents are becoming the substrate through which problems get solved — and the durability of any startup we back now depends on whether its founder is solving the problem as agents will reshape it, not as it exists today. Alongside Structural Discontinuity and Memory Lock-in, every TechGroup deal now answers a third question: does this company's value, model, and moat survive — or strengthen — when agents become the predominant actor and consumer of digital products, work, and transactions?
01
Why now — what just happened
  • The internet crossed over to the machines — a year early. June 2026, Cloudflare: bots passed 57.5% of all web traffic. Agentic traffic grew nearly 8,000% in one year and runs ~1,000:1 — one human intention spawns a thousand machine requests.
  • The builders say knowledge work is next. Boris Cherny (Anthropic): coding is largely solved — 100% of his code agent-written, 10–30 PRs/day directing fleets; ~100× more people directing work than doing it. Karpathy: the decade of agents, autonomy advancing a notch at a time across every function.
  • Grassroots demand is already here. OpenClaw — an autonomous agent living in your messaging apps that acts for you — earned 100k GitHub stars in 48 hours. The interface flips: you stop operating software and start delegating.
  • The boardroom has conceded. Bret Taylor (Chair of OpenAI): "the vast majority of the digital interactions you'll have with your customers will be via your agent." The Economist is building a two-track internet — one for humans, one for agents. When incumbents restructure around the question, the question is settled.
02
The real test — agents reimagine problems, not interfaces

The shallow version asks "is the product built for humans or bots?" — an interface checklist. The real test runs one level deeper: does a future of capable agents reimagine, dissolve, or transform the problem this startup solves — and is the founder building for that world or this one? The doorway question on every deal:

"Is this information for a human to act on, or a transaction for an agent to complete?"

A beautiful comparison-shopping site is an excellent answer to "a human needs to compare and decide." In the agent era the human doesn't browse — their agent queries structured offers and transacts. The problem itself dissolves: unmonetized bot traffic, collapsed affiliate funnel. The founder who sees this builds the offer-and-transaction layer agents call, priced per completed transaction. Same domain, reimagined problem — durable. The first one is roadkill with good design.

03
Where it bites first
  • Work & the firm — the unit of production flips from human-hour to agent-task. A 10-person agent-native company delivers what a 1,000-person firm did. Per-seat SaaS pricing is exposed at the model level — when agents do the work, seats stop counting anything real. Live, not slideware: Bret Taylor's Sierra charges only when the agent resolves the problem. Headcount-heavy operating plans start reading as liabilities — the impressive answer to "how will you scale the team?" may be "we won't."
  • Commerce & markets — the buyer becomes an agent. Discovery, comparison, purchase run through a program that wants structured facts and the best transaction — not ads or brand affect. Attention-middle layers get routed around; value re-concentrates at the transactional plumbing and the source of supply. And the 1,000:1 load ratio is a P&L event: monetize per human view while serving per agent request and a cost-revenue scissors opens underneath you.
04
The four postures — half the verdict is naming where a company stands
PostureDefinitionVerdict logic
❌ Threatened Agents reimagine or dissolve the problem it solves. Excellent solution for a world that is ending. Default toward pass. Good design makes it more dangerous — it hides the rot.
✅ Riding The company is the reimagined solution. The prize. Verify it is real, not narrated.
✅ Enabling Picks-and-shovels — the rails agents run on. Often the most underwritable bet; wins regardless of app-layer winners.
✅ Insulated Genuinely un-disruptable — physical, high-trust, human-verified. Durable, but test hard. Most "insulated" companies are Threatened and don't know it.

The most important discrimination this lens forces: a company Riding the shift vs. one Threatened by it but telling a Riding story. A polished "AI-powered" deck is frequently a Threatened company wearing Riding's clothes.

05
What stays durable — the affirmative side

Un-scrapable value (physical, high-trust human relationships) · flywheels that compound under agent load (data that gets richer, not cheaper, as machine usage scales) · transactional plumbing (paid when an agent acts, not when a person looks) · intelligent / physical infrastructure (the rails, not the ride) · human-verification & authenticity premiums · governance of memory (context expiration, forgetting, control over what agents retain). And Taylor's incumbent-side sharpening: when agents can generate the software, value migrates from production to trust — the customer buys the accumulated hardening and institutional trust no competitor can mint overnight.
