## Where Jamie Thinks We Need To Start From

Not *"which platform do we build?"* That's a Phase-2 question.

**The Phase-1 question is: what is NWA's strategic identity, and what problems are we solving?**

Two candidate answers sit on the table:

1. **Business #1 — Deal discovery at scale.** Keep optimizing the firehose. Faster intake, better screening memo, member matching as a feature. (Jessica's V1 defaults to this.)
2. **Business #2 — Domain-expertise investor network.** Curated / referred / cross-syndicate sourcing; member expertise graph as the platform; deals flow *across* a member-centric substrate. (The Reframe.)

These are not additive. They produce different products. The four-person huddle is the place to decide.

## Jamie's Current Read (ideas, not conclusions)

- The edge is *judgment*, not *filtering*. NWA's 200 members across 6 verticals are the asset. Any product design that treats them as a feature rather than the spine under-uses the moat.
- Ron's "and-and-also" is right at the track level: **Sourcing · Member Intelligence · Diligence · Syndication** are all real and all interlocking.
- The Reframe's 3-layer architecture (**build where we differentiate, integrate where we don't**) is right at the build-posture level. Layer 1 integrate; Layer 2 build; Layer 3 lift from CoWork.
- **Tracks and layers are not the same thing.** Tracks are *what problems NWA solves* (outside-facing). Layers are *how we build the stack* (inside-facing). They reconcile — see the GUT draft.
- The unifying substrate across all four tracks is a **member expertise graph + decisions ledger**. That is the one thing no vendor can sell us and everything else plugs into.
