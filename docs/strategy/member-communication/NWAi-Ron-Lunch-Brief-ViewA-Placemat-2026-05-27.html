The Placemat (point-and-show)
01 · BUILT
What we've built to date
The analytical layer Dealum lacks
  • Working end-to-end deal pipeline: 7 stages · 8 commands · 9 AI agents · 10 proprietary frameworks
  • Single user. One deal at a time. Real deals through to scored DD Reports + Investment Memos.
  • Filesystem-first; Dealum overlay only. Three surfaces (Desktop, CLI, Cowork).
02 · BUILD
What we need to build
Deal intel + member intel · web
  • V1 (30–45 days): two tracks at once — deal intel (the 9 agents on the web) + member intel substrate (graph, ledger, Match Engine, social deal card).
  • V2 (~3 months): onboard Medical · Space · Consumer · Industrial · Fintech. 2 weeks per group.
  • V3+ ecosystem sourcing. Dealum stays through V2; platform subsumes Beyond V3.
03 · ENTERPRISE-GRADE
Desktop tool → always-on platform
Four dimensions: reliability · multi-user · Dealum · security
  • From session-dependent desktop → cloud always-on. Deals processed when they enter Dealum, not when I'm at my Mac.
  • Multi-user: right output to right member at right stage. Structured SME input replaces email + ad-hoc.
  • Bidirectional Dealum integration. Encryption + RBAC + append-only audit. Member data rights from day one.
04 · THE MOAT
Member intelligence platform
Beyond deals — the compounding asset
  • Deal intelligence is the wedge. Member intelligence is the asset.
  • Substrate = Sensors + Member Graph + Decisions Ledger + Policy + Learning Loop. Append-only; compounds.
  • 200 members · 6 groups · 20+ years of indexed IC reasoning. No vendor can sell us this. No competitor can copy it.
  • AI is the connective tissue. The substrate is the moat.
